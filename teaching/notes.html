Session 1: shared-bot run sheet

Use the bot only for S1-01B. One team drives while two teams do the same lesson in simulation, then rotate. S1-02 and optional S1-03 are simulator-only for all teams. Deploy once during S1-01B; the transfer takes about 15 seconds after Run.

Rotation rules

  1. For two-person teams, alternate driver and navigator during the S1-01B bot turn; the instructor or a neighboring student can be the observer/debugger.
  2. For the three-person team, use driver, navigator, and observer/debugger, then rotate each role during the S1-01B bot turn.
  3. Use a visible timer and announce two-minute and one-minute warnings. The next team waits outside the practice area until the current team presses STOP.
  4. Keep the bot turn focused on behavior. All code changes happen in the simulator.
