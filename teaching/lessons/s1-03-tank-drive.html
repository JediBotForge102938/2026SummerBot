S1-03 · Arcade drive to tank drive

Change the control model so each stick drives one side of the robot.

Course mapMissionResourcesInstructor notes
Optional simulator exercise

Extend S1-01 by replacing arcade control with tank control.

Goal

Replace arcade control with tank control:

StickControls
Left stick YLeft front and left back wheels
Right stick YRight front and right back wheels

Exercise

  1. Run [S1-03] Tank Drive - Exercise in the simulator.
  2. Confirm it starts with S1-01 arcade control: left stick Y drives forward/backward and right stick X turns.
  3. Open S1_03_TankDriveExerciseTeleOp.java.
  4. Find the forward/rotate calculations in loop(). Replace the arcade left/right formulas with independent left and right inputs: left stick Y for the left side and right stick Y for the right side.
  5. Run the test again. Drive straight with both sticks, turn by moving the sticks in opposite directions, and curve by moving them different amounts.

Compare

Open [S1-03] Tank Drive - Solution after making your edit. The solution uses the same motor directions and side grouping, with independent tank inputs.

Takeaway

Tank drive assigns an independent command to each side. The four motors still run as two groups, but the driver controls those groups with two sticks instead of combining forward and rotation.
