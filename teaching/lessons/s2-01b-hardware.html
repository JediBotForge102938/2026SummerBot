S2-01B · Drive the mecanum robot

Repeat the mecanum tests on the physical robot after practicing in the simulator.

Course mapMissionResourcesInstructor notes
Mecanum hardware practice

The instructor has already prepared the robot configuration. Your job is to select the mecanum lesson OpMode and compare the physical robot with the simulator.

Prepare the robot

  1. Confirm that the Robot Controller app is already deployed and the Driver Station is connected.
  2. Choose the preconfigured drive profile and select [S2-01] Mecanum.
  3. Connect the gamepad as gamepad1.

Test one motion at a time

  1. Place the robot in a clear, safe practice area and wait for instructor approval.
  2. Press INIT, then START.
  3. Test forward/back, strafe, and rotate separately. Release the controls between tests.
  4. Compare each physical result with the simulator result. Tell your team what matched and what differed.
  5. Press STOP before changing the OpMode or moving the robot.

Takeaway

The same wheel-power program can be tested in two environments. A simulator observation is useful when it predicts what the physical robot will do, but safe physical testing is how you confirm the real drivetrain.

If the robot moves unexpectedly, release the controls and press STOP. Do not try to correct a moving robot by hand.
