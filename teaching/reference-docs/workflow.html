Quick reference

Run, observe, explain.

  1. Open the simulator or connect the Driver Station and Robot Controller.
  2. Select the exact teaching opmode name.
  3. Press INIT, then START.
  4. Before each test, tell your team what you expect to happen.
  5. Drive slowly and observe the robot and telemetry.
  6. Press STOP before changing hardware or configuration.
  7. Tell your team and instructor what happened and what you would test next.

The original StarterBot files remain untouched references. Teaching files are isolated so one experiment cannot break another.
